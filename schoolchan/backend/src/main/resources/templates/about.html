About SchoolChan

An anonymous, account-free message board for St. Joseph's Academy of Las Piñas Senior High School.

What it is

SchoolChan is a plain bulletin board: you pick a board, you start a thread, you reply. There are no public profiles, no follower counts, and no likes that turn into a score. Every post is signed Anonymous.

Threads are never deleted. When a thread gets pushed off the front of a board it moves to the archive, where it stays readable forever. Quiet threads are not punished.

How posting works

  • Greentext — start a line with > and it turns green, like other boards.
  • Quotes — type >>123 to link to another post, and hover it to preview.
  • Sage — tick the box to reply without bumping the thread back to the top.
  • Spoilers — tag your post so other people can choose to read it.
  • Images — attach a picture. It is checked before anyone else sees it.
  • Votes and reports — vote on what is useful, and report what should not be here.

Moderation

Nothing is removed by a machine. Every post is published straight away. A keyword list and a language model look for harassment, hate speech, threats, explicit images and scam links, and a student or a moderator can report a post too. When that happens the post is pointed out to the staff team, where a person reads it and decides whether it stays.

Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. If a post reads like a real safety risk, it goes to a restricted staff queue where a person decides what happens. See the rules for the full description.

Your privacy

We store as little as possible. A random cookie identifies your browser so votes and bookmarks work, and only a hash of it is kept. The network address a post came from is encrypted and stored separately, is only ever opened by staff during a real safety investigation, and is deleted automatically after 90 days. It is never shown on the board and never attached to a post.

You can read the plain-language version of this on the rules page.

Who is behind it

SchoolChan is a capstone project: a school board that keeps its anonymous, archival feel while adding the moderation and safety tooling a real school would need.
