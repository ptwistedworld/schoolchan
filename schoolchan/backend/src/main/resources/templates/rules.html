======= Rules — SchoolChan

Rules and conditions

These are the rules everyone agrees to by posting. They are short on purpose. If something is unclear, ask a moderator through the board.

Rules

Be decent to each other

Argue with ideas, not people. No bullying, no shaming, no posting anyone's real name, address, schedule, or photos without permission.

Keep it school-appropriate

No sexual content, no graphic violence, no drugs, no gambling, no scams or phishing links. Tag spoilers as spoilers.

How moderation works

Every post is published straight away. Nothing is removed automatically. A filter, another student, or a moderator may point a post out to the staff team, and then a person reads it and decides whether it stays. If a post is taken down it stays readable to its author under My posts, and the author can ask a moderator to look again.

Escalation — when a post is a safety concern

If a post reads like a genuine safety risk to the poster or to the school, it is hidden straight away and put in a restricted staff queue. A staff member may then forward the post, and the poster's network address, to the school administration or guidance office so a human can act. This is only for genuine safety risks — never for ordinary disagreements. Nothing is sent automatically; a person always decides.

What we store, and for how long

A random cookie identifies your browser so votes and bookmarks work; only a hash of it is kept. The network address a post came from is encrypted, stored separately from the post, and deleted automatically after 90 days unless it is attached to an open safety case. Student sign-in is only a gate: it is never linked to anything posted.

What you can do here

  • Browse the boards and read a thread.
  • Sign in with your student ID to post, reply, vote, report, and bookmark.
  • Open My posts to see what you wrote, including anything a moderator removed, and to send an appeal.
  • Open Bookmarks for the threads you saved.
  • See a post that breaks a rule? Use the Report button under that post. Reports are anonymous and read by staff.
  • Want an account for a classmate? Ask staff to onboard a student ID.
  • Lost your password? Use a reset code.
  • Moderator or admin? Staff sign in.

Reports are read by people, in the order they arrive. A report never identifies you to the person who wrote the post.
